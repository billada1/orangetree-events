On Saturday 29 April 2017 Yılmaz Erdoğan brought Münaşaka to the Seymour Centre in Sydney.

Erdoğan is one of Türkiye’s best-loved storytellers: actor, director, poet and the writer behind Vizontele and Ekşi Elmalar. Münaşaka is his one-man show, an evening of stories, poems and sharp observations about everyday life, families and the distance between Türkiye and the places its people now call home.

For a Sydney audience that had grown up with his films, it was a rare chance to hear him live, in Turkish.

Orange Tree Events presented the Sydney show.
