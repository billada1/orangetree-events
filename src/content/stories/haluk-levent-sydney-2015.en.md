On Saturday 7 November 2015 Haluk Levent played La Luna Lounge in Sydney.

Levent has been one of the defining voices of Turkish rock since the 1990s, with songs like Elfida and Yollarda Bulurum Seni. At home he is just as well known for AHBAP, the volunteer network he founded, which has led relief work after floods, fires and the 2023 earthquakes.

It was one of the first nights Orange Tree Events supported.
