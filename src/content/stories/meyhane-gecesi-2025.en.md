On Saturday 1 November 2025 Orange Tree Events and ATAM turned the Sapphire Function Centre in Auburn into a meyhane for the night.

A meyhane is a Turkish tavern: long tables, meze and the old songs everyone knows the words to. Meyhane Gecesi brought that format to a Sydney function room for one evening.

The music came from Nida Rock, the Sydney band that moves easily between meyhane şarkıları, Anatolian rock and Turkish classics. The night sold out.

Our special guest that night was Nihat Ziyalan, the Yeşilçam actor and poet. Born in Adana in 1936, he played in many Turkish films of the 1960s and 70s, then made Sydney his home in 1980 and kept writing poetry here. He passed away in Sydney on 11 October 2026, aged 90. We are grateful he shared this night with us, and remember him with love and respect.

Orange Tree Events presented Meyhane Gecesi: Nida Makamında with ATAM.
