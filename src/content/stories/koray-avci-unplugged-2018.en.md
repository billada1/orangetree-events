On Thursday 25 January 2018 Koray Avcı played an unplugged night at Bankstown Sports Club.

Avcı made his name busking on the streets of Istanbul before his versions of Anatolian folk songs, from Hoşçakal to Ankaranın Bağları, became some of the most played Turkish music of the decade. An acoustic set suits him best: a voice, a guitar and songs the audience already knows by heart.

Orange Tree Events presented the night.
