After the earthquakes of 6 February 2023 in Türkiye and Syria, Sydney’s Turkish community turned grief into something useful: nights out that raised money for survivors.

At the Enmore Theatre, Tahir Bilgiç and Turkuaz put on a benefit night of comedians and magicians. Orange Tree Events promoted and supported it, including a television spot on Channel 9 News.

On Sunday 2 April, local artists came together at Club Burwood RSL for a Türkiye Earthquake Relief Benefit Concert. Kral Music ft Kadir, Levent & Özkan and the Austolian Dance Academy were on the line-up, and we helped spread the word.
