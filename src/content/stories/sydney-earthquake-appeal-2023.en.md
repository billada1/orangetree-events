After the earthquakes of 6 February 2023 in Türkiye and Syria, Sydney’s Turkish community turned grief into something useful: nights out that raised money for survivors.

At the Enmore Theatre, Tahir Bilgiç hosted a benefit night of comedians and magicians with Turkuaz. Orange Tree Events helped promote and run it.

On Sunday 2 April, Turkish Association NSW brought local artists together at Club Burwood RSL for a Türkiye Earthquake Relief Benefit Concert. Kral Music ft Kadir, Levent & Özkan and the Austolian Dance Academy were on the line-up, and we helped spread the word.
