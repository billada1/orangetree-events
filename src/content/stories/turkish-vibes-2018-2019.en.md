Turkish Vibes was a dance night that mixed old-school R&B with Turkish dance hits.

The first night was Saturday 13 October 2018 at the Coolibah Hotel in Merrylands. Guest DJ Ilka (Ilker Aydın) and DJ Göksel Ada shared the decks.

It came back on Saturday 2 November 2019, doors at 7pm, same room and same pairing. This time it sold out.

The two DJs went on to play our New Year’s Eve nights with ATAM, and DJ Ilka has opened shows for maNga and Eypio. Turkish Vibes is where that partnership began.

Orange Tree Events presented both nights.
