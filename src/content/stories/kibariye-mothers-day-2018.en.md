On Sunday 13 May 2018, Mother’s Day, Kibariye sang at The Renaissance in Lidcombe. Doors opened at 6pm and Orange Tree Events presented the night.

Kibariye is one of Türkiye’s best-loved voices in arabesque and fantezi, the emotional, string-led popular music that many Turkish families in Sydney grew up with. Her concert was a Mother’s Day night out, with dinner tables in the Renaissance’s function room.

Orange Tree Events presented the concert.
