One night only, a full band, and a room that knew every word. On 15 December 2018 İlyas Yalçıntaş brought his live show to Max Watt's at the Entertainment Quarter, and Sydney turned out for it.

The VIP meet and greet sold out before the night. VIP guests came in early to meet him and have a photo taken, and the rest of the crowd queued along the building for the 7:30 doors.

Before he came on, the Summer Turkish Vibes club night warmed up the room: Turkish funk band Funkatolia, guest DJ Ilker Aydın from Bodrum and DJ Göksel Ada. The show itself started around eight.

It was an all-ages night: under 18s had the mezzanine to themselves, with a non-alcohol bar, so whole families could come.
