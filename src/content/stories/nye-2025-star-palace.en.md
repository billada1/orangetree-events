On New Year’s Eve 2024, ATAM and Orange Tree Events welcomed 2025 together at Star Palace Reception in Fairfield.

Tahir Bilgiç, Habib from Pizza and Housos, hosted the night. With the microphone in hand he had the room, and the musicians behind him, laughing.

Halil & Özkan played with a live orchestra, and DJ Gurbz kept the dance floor full until late. Families danced hand in hand in front of the stage, under the “Happy New Year 2025” banner.

Tables of ten shared a full meze spread and a three-course dinner, with beer and wine, arcade games for the children and raffle prizes. It was a family fundraiser for ATAM.

Thank you to everyone who danced into 2025 with us.
