Turkish rock came to The Star on Sunday 23 April 2017. Teoman Rockfest brought Teoman to the Marquee with three guests: Cahit Berkay, a founder of Moğollar, Moğollar’s lead singer Emrah Karaca, and Tibet Ağırtan of Mavi Sakal.

The tour played Crown in Melbourne the night before. Sydney fell on 23 Nisan, National Sovereignty and Children’s Day in Türkiye, which gave the night a second reason to celebrate.

Orange Tree Events presented the Sydney show.
